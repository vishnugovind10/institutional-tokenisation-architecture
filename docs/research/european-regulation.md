# European regulation

## Architecture question

Which legal category applies to the instrument, issuer, service, institution, participants and activities in each jurisdiction?

## Design implications

- DORA [SRC-001] is a primary EU digital operational resilience text; map ICT, incident, testing and third-party requirements only after confirming the entity/activity is in scope.
- MiCA [SRC-002] concerns specified crypto-assets and service providers; it does not by itself classify every tokenized deposit or financial instrument.
- The DLT Pilot Regime [SRC-003] is a defined authorization framework for eligible DLT market infrastructures.
- Establish legal register, instrument classification, custody, settlement and customer-claim treatment with authorized counsel.
- Recheck consolidated law, delegated acts, national implementation and supervisory guidance before relying on dates or obligations.

## Sources and caveats

Sources are linked to primary EUR-Lex legal texts. DORA applies from 17 January 2025 under Article 64 [SRC-001]. MiCA includes phased application dates under Article 149 [SRC-002]. The DLT Pilot Regime has its own application provisions [SRC-003]. This workbench is not legal advice and makes no compliance determination.
